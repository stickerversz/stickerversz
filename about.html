Our story

Stickers built to outlast the hype.

StickerVersz is a Moroccan sticker brand for people who wear their passions on their gear.

Why StickerVersz

Most stickers fade, peel, or dissolve at the first splash. Ours don't. Every StickerVersz design is printed on high-quality waterproof vinyl with a laminate finish, so the colors stay sharp through rain, spills, and daily wear.

We keep the catalogue tight and curated across seven universes — anime, K-pop, gaming, sports, movies, music, and study — and we drop new designs regularly. Every sticker gets its own serial number, which makes ordering as simple as sending a DM.

How to order

No checkout, no accounts. Your order lives in one Instagram DM.

  1. Browse all stickers

    Explore the collection and shortlist the designs you love.

  2. Note the serial numbers

    Each sticker has a unique ID — tap "Copy ID" to grab it instantly.

  3. Send the serial numbers via Instagram DM

    Paste your list into a message to @stickerversz.

  4. Receive confirmation

    We confirm availability, total, and delivery details.

  5. Enjoy your stickers

    Slap them on your laptop, bottle, or notebook — rain-proof, spill-proof, life-proof.
